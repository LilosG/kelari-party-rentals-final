One of the most common things people tell us after their first party rental experience: "I didn't realize how much I needed to think about." Not in a bad way — just that there are more moving pieces than expected, and having a list upfront makes the whole process much smoother.

This checklist is designed for parties in Carlsbad, Encinitas, Oceanside, San Marcos, and surrounding North County San Diego communities. It's organized by category so you can work through it systematically, with guest count guidance so you're not guessing on quantities.

## Start Here: The Four Questions That Drive Everything

Before you look at a single item, answer these four questions. They determine almost everything about what you need.

**1. How many guests?** This is your anchor number. Every quantity calculation flows from here.

**2. Indoor or outdoor?** Outdoor events need shade coverage. Indoor events don't, but may need different table configurations.

**3. Seated dinner or cocktail-style?** Seated events need one chair per guest at a table. Cocktail events can use fewer seats (roughly 60–70% of guest count) with high-top tables mixed in.

**4. What type of event?** A casual backyard birthday has different needs than a baby shower or graduation party. Milestone events typically call for more linens, tableware, and décor.

## The Checklist: Tables

Tables are the foundation. Get this right and everything else falls into place.

**Round tables (60-inch, seat 8–10):**
Great for conversation — guests face each other. Best for showers, engagement parties, and milestone birthdays.
- 20 guests: 3 tables
- 30 guests: 4 tables
- 50 guests: 7 tables
- 75 guests: 9–10 tables
- 100 guests: 13 tables

**Rectangular tables (6-foot, seat 6–8):**
More efficient use of space, better for long setups and park events.
- 20 guests: 3 tables
- 30 guests: 5 tables
- 50 guests: 8 tables

**Always add:** 1–2 extra tables for food, cake, gifts, or a beverage station. These are separate from your seating count.

## The Checklist: Chairs

Simple math: one chair per guest. Then add a buffer.

- 20 guests: 22–24 chairs
- 30 guests: 33–35 chairs
- 50 guests: 55 chairs
- 75 guests: 80–82 chairs
- 100 guests: 108–110 chairs

**Chair type by event:**
- Casual birthday, park party, backyard BBQ → standard folding chairs
- Baby shower, engagement party, milestone birthday → padded or Chiavari chairs
- Kids' party → mix of adult and kids' folding chairs

For [birthday party rentals](/services/birthday-party-rentals/) and [baby shower rentals](/services/baby-shower-rentals/), chair style makes a significant visual difference. It's worth the small upgrade.

## The Checklist: Shade and Shelter

For any outdoor event in North County San Diego, this category is non-negotiable during daylight hours.

**Pop-up canopy (10x10):** Covers 1–2 tables. Good for a small food station or bar area.

**Pop-up canopy (10x20):** Covers 3–4 tables. The most common choice for small backyard parties of 20–30 guests.

**Frame tent (20x20):** Covers 6–8 tables. Right size for 40–50 guests at a graduation or shower.

**Frame tent (20x40):** Covers 12–16 tables. For larger events of 75–100 guests.

For [park party rentals](/services/park-party-rentals/) at spots like Poinsettia Park or Alga Norte in Carlsbad, or Encinitas Community Park, always include a tent. Parks have open exposure — shade doesn't happen by accident.

## The Checklist: Linens

Linens are the single highest-impact, lowest-cost upgrade you can make. A bare folding table looks like a cafeteria. The same table with a tablecloth looks intentional.

**Tablecloths:** One per table. For round tables, make sure you're ordering the right size (90-inch round for 60-inch tables, or floor-length for a more formal look).

**Table runners:** Optional but add a layer of visual interest. One per table.

**Napkins:** One per guest plus 10–15% extra. Go with cloth for showers and milestone events; printed paper napkins work fine for casual parties.

**Color coordination:** Pick two colors — a base and an accent. Keep tablecloths in the base color and runners/napkins in the accent. This works every time without overthinking it.

## The Checklist: Tableware

This is where you make the call between disposable and rented.

**Rented tableware (plates, flatware, glassware):** Worth it for baby showers, engagement parties, graduation parties with a sit-down meal, and any event where the presentation matters. Eliminates mid-party trash management and dramatically elevates the table setting.

**Disposable:** Totally fine for casual backyard birthdays, park parties, and events where guests are moving around. Use quality paper plates and skip the flimsy plastic forks.

**Per-person quantities for rented tableware:**
- Dinner plates: 1 per guest + 5% extra
- Salad/dessert plates: 1 per guest
- Forks and knives: 1 set per guest + 5% extra
- Water glasses: 1–2 per guest
- Wine or champagne glasses: 1 per guest (if serving)

## The Checklist: Décor

Décor is entirely optional but makes a real difference for milestone events. A few items that consistently make the biggest visual impact:

**Centerpieces:** One per table. Can be floral, candle-based, or decorative objects. For [baby shower rentals](/services/baby-shower-rentals/) and [engagement party rentals](/services/engagement-party-rentals/), centerpieces are worth prioritizing.

**Backdrop:** A single backdrop behind the main table or food station photographs beautifully and defines the focal point of the event. More impactful per dollar than almost anything else.

**String lights:** For evening events or covered tent areas, string lights are the most effective and affordable ambiance upgrade available.

**Balloon arch or clusters:** High visual impact, especially for birthdays and graduation parties. Works at both park and backyard events.

## The Checklist: Logistics

A few things people forget until the day of:

**Delivery window:** Confirm the delivery and setup window with your rental company. Most companies need 1–2 hours before your event start time. Make sure someone is at the venue to receive the delivery.

**Park permits:** If you're hosting at a public park in Carlsbad, Encinitas, Oceanside, or San Marcos, check permit requirements with your city's parks department before booking. Most parks require advance notice and a fee for events with equipment.

**Power access:** If you're using lighting or any powered equipment, confirm power source location before setup day.

**Pickup timing:** Confirm post-event pickup time so you're not waiting around. With Kelari, we coordinate all of this at booking — no surprises on the day.
