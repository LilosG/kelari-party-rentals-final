Graduation season in North County San Diego hits fast. One week you're watching the ceremony dates get announced, the next it's two weeks out and you're scrambling to find tables, a tent, and enough chairs for 80 people.

This timeline is designed to prevent that. It walks through exactly what to do and when — from the moment you know the graduation date through the day of the party — for families in Carlsbad, Encinitas, Oceanside, San Marcos, and surrounding communities.

## 8 Weeks Out: Lock In the Basics

Eight weeks feels early. It isn't — not for June graduations in North County San Diego.

**Confirm the graduation date and ceremony details.** Most local high schools announce ceremony dates in March or April. The moment you have the date, your planning clock starts.

**Decide on venue.** Backyard or park? This decision drives everything else. Backyards give you more control and flexibility. Parks give you more space but require permit reservations — which can take 2–4 weeks to process. If you're considering a park like Poinsettia Park or Alga Norte in Carlsbad, or Encinitas Community Park, start the permit process now.

**Estimate your guest count.** You don't need a final headcount yet, but you need a working number. Are you planning for 30 people or 100? This determines tent size, table count, and package tier.

**Set your budget.** Full-service graduation party rentals for 50 guests in North County San Diego typically run $400–$700 including delivery, setup, and pickup. For 100 guests, budget $800–$1,400. Knowing your number upfront prevents scope creep.

## 4 Weeks Out: Finalize the Details

By four weeks out, your rentals should be booked. Now you're filling in the details.

**Finalize your guest list and send invitations.** Digital invites via text or email are perfectly standard for graduation parties. Give guests at least 3 weeks notice so they can plan around competing June events.

**Confirm your rental order details.** Review your rental package — table count, chair count, tent size, linen colors. If your headcount has changed significantly since booking, contact your rental company to adjust. Most can accommodate changes up to 2 weeks before the event.

**Plan your food and beverage setup.** Will you cater, cook, or do a hybrid? For large graduation parties, a taco bar or BBQ setup works well and scales easily. Plan for a dedicated food table (separate from guest seating) and a beverage station.

**Order any personalized items.** Graduation banners, custom centerpieces, and school-colored décor items often need 2–3 weeks for production and shipping. Order now if you want them ready in time.

## 2 Weeks Out: Confirm Everything

Two weeks out is your confirmation checkpoint.

**Confirm your rental delivery window.** We'll reach out to confirm your setup time, but it's worth a quick check-in. For graduation parties, we typically aim to deliver 2 hours before your event start time to ensure everything is perfectly set up before guests arrive.

**Confirm park permit approval** if applicable. If you haven't received confirmation on your park reservation, follow up with your city's parks department now.

**Finalize your food order** with any caterers or restaurants. June weekends book out for catering just like they do for rentals.

**Create a simple day-of timeline.** What time does rental delivery start? What time do guests arrive? What time does the graduate get home from the ceremony? Write it down and share it with anyone helping coordinate.

## 1 Week Out: Prep Your Space

**Measure and prep your backyard.** If you're hosting at home, walk your yard with the tent footprint in mind. Clear any furniture, planters, or obstacles from the setup area. Note any overhead obstacles — trees, power lines, patio covers — and mention them to your rental team.

**Confirm headcount one more time.** Send a quick reminder to guests who haven't responded. Your final number affects food quantities and any last-minute chair adjustments.

**Charge any devices you'll use for music.** A Bluetooth speaker or outdoor sound system makes a huge difference at a backyard graduation party. Test it now so you're not troubleshooting it the morning of.

## Day Before: Final Checks

**Confirm rental delivery time** with a quick call or text. Know the exact arrival window.

**Prep your food station area.** Decide where the food table goes, where the beverage station lives, and where the cake or dessert will be displayed. Having this planned before setup day makes the whole process faster.

**Brief anyone helping.** If family members are helping with setup, food, or greeting guests, give them a quick rundown of the timeline so everyone's on the same page.
