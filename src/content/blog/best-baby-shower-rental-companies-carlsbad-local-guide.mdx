## Planning Your Carlsbad Baby Shower: Practical Tips

**Book Early**

Spring and early summer (April–June) fill fast. Aim for 4–6 weeks out. If your date is sooner, reach out—we sometimes have flexibility.

**Know Your Guest Count**

This drives table and chair quantity. A rough count is fine at first; we finalize closer to the date.

**Choose Your Venue First**

Backyard or park? This shapes rental recommendations. Parks require permits (Carlsbad Parks & Rec handles these). Backyards need space assessment.

**Discuss Color & Style**

Soft pastels (ivory, blush, sage)? Bold brights? Neutral? Tell us your vision. We'll propose linens, runners, and chair combinations that work.

**Plan for Weather**

Carlsbad's coastal climate is mild year-round, but June can bring June Gloom. If you're planning a park shower, discuss backup timing or tent rental with us.

**Consider Delivery Timing**

For morning showers, we deliver early (7–8 a.m.). For afternoon events, we coordinate to minimize setup time before guests arrive.
