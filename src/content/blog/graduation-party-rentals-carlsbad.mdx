## Where to Host Your Graduation Party

### Poinsettia Park (The Carlsbad Staple)

Poinsettia Park is the go-to for Carlsbad grads. Huge lawn, good parking, and those picnic areas work perfectly for rentals. You'll want to reserve the park early—like January or February for June graduations. Once you've got your park slot locked, call us for table and chair rentals. We deliver, set up, and handle breakdown.

The park fits groups from 50 to 200+ depending on which section you book. Bring your own food or hire a caterer—either way, our white resin folding chairs and 8ft banquet tables create a clean, party-ready setup.

### Alga Norte Park (Carlsbad's Hidden Gem)

Smaller than Poinsettia but way less crowded. Alga Norte works beautifully for intimate grad parties (30-60 people). The tree coverage is solid, so you're not roasting in the June sun. Parking's tighter, but the vibe is more relaxed. Perfect if you want a family-focused celebration without the chaos.

### Oceanside Parks (Stagecoach & Holiday Park)

If your grad goes to Oceanside High or Rancho Buena Vista, Stagecoach Park and Holiday Park are your backyard. Stagecoach has great open space and is popular with larger groups. Holiday Park is more intimate. Both are easy to access, and we deliver rentals to both regularly.

### Encinitas Venues (Hospice Grove & Calavera Hills)

Hosting in Encinitas? Hospice Grove and Calavera Hills are solid options. Hospice Grove has shade and that North County charm. Calavera Hills is newer with better facilities. Both work for 40-150 person celebrations.

### Backyard Parties (The Carlsbad Favorite)

Not everyone wants a park. Plenty of Carlsbad families host in their own backyards—especially in neighborhoods like Bressi Ranch and Aviara where homes have serious outdoor space. A backyard celebration feels more intimate, and you control the whole vibe. We deliver and set up the same way we do at parks. Check your HOA rules first (some have restrictions on rentals), then give us a call.

## Table & Chair Setup for Different Party Styles

Your rental setup depends on how you want guests to experience the party. Here's what we recommend:

### Seated Dinner Setup

Going traditional? Plan for 8-10 six-foot banquet tables for 75 guests. Pair them with our white resin folding chairs for a clean, classic look. Add 90"x156" polyester tablecloths in white or black, and you've got an elegant foundation. Upgrade to wood Chiavari chairs with 2" ivory cushions if you want something more upscale—we've got 100 of them in stock.

### Cocktail-Style Mingling

Smaller, standing-room celebration? Use 30" round cocktail tables (we have 30 in stock) at 30" or 42" height. Mix in some 60" round tables for food or a gift table. Dress them with spandex covers in white, black, or tan-ivory for a polished look. This setup works great for 50-100 guests who want to move around.

### Hybrid Approach

Some of the best grad parties we've seen use both. Seated tables for dinner, then cocktail tables pushed to the side once the sun sets and dancing starts. It's flexible, feels modern, and guests appreciate the options.

## Linens & Styling That Matter

Table linens are where you set the tone. Here's what we see work best for grad parties:

**White polyester** feels fresh and timeless. Works for any theme, any venue, any vibe. 90"x132" for 6ft tables, 90"x156" for 8ft tables, 120" round for floor-length elegance on 60" round tables.

**Black linens** add sophistication. Perfect if you're leaning into a more formal celebration or if you want contrast with white chairs.

**Tan-ivory** is underrated. Warm, natural, and photographs beautifully—especially at sunset in Carlsbad.

**Runners** add personality without overdoing it. We stock 10ft runners in tan, green, pink, baby blue, and off-white. Lay one down the center of a banquet table or drape it across cocktail tables. It's a small detail that guests actually notice.

**Spandex covers** transform basic tables instantly. Our 60" round spandex in white or black, 30" cocktail spandex in white/black/tan-ivory, and 6ft/8ft banquet spandex all dress up rentals and hide wear. They're especially useful if you're renting tables from multiple sources or if your venue tables aren't in great shape.

## Common Mistakes We See (And How to Avoid Them)

**Booking too late.** June graduations book out by April. If your grad just decided to celebrate, call us immediately. We might have inventory, but we can't promise it.

**Underestimating table count.** "75 guests" doesn't mean 6 tables. Factor in food tables, gift tables, maybe a DJ table. We usually recommend 10-12 tables for 75 people when you account for everything.

**Forgetting about weather.** Carlsbad June is usually perfect, but a breeze can move napkins and tablecloths. We can help secure linens, or you can rent a tent separately. Just plan for it.

**Not checking park rules.** Some parks have noise ordinances, parking limits, or restrictions on alcohol. Know your venue's rules before you finalize your rental plan.

**Mixing mismatched rentals.** If you're renting chairs from us but tables from someone else, make sure they'll actually work together. We can advise on compatibility—just ask.
