## Understanding Baby Shower Rental Costs in Bressi Ranch

Baby shower rental costs break down into three main categories: **furniture (tables and chairs)**, **linens and decor**, and **delivery/setup**. In Bressi Ranch, you're typically looking at a total package between $500 and $1,500 depending on guest count and style choices.

Here's the honest truth: a smaller, intimate shower (20–30 guests) costs significantly less than a larger celebration (75+ guests). But the per-person cost actually *improves* as you add more tables and chairs, because delivery and setup fees stay the same.

Bressi Ranch properties often have beautiful outdoor spaces, which means you can take advantage of natural scenery and skip some pricier decor. That's a real advantage compared to indoor venues that need more visual impact.

## Breaking Down Furniture Costs

**Tables** are your biggest expense. A 60-inch round table—perfect for seating 5–6 guests comfortably—costs around $75–$95 per table for a half-day rental. For a baby shower with 30 guests, you'd need five tables, bringing furniture alone to roughly $375–$475.

If you prefer a longer, more intimate feel, an 8-foot banquet table seats 8–10 guests and runs about $65–$85 per rental. Mixing table sizes is totally fine and often looks more intentional than a room full of identical rounds.

**Chairs** are where you can make real style choices. Our white resin folding chairs are durable workhorses at $5–$8 per chair. They're practical and photograph well. If you want something more elegant, light fruitwood chiavari chairs with ivory cushions run $12–$18 per chair—a noticeable upgrade that reads as "special occasion" without breaking the budget.

For 30 guests with five 60-inch round tables, you'd rent 30 chairs. White folding chairs would cost $150–$240. Chiavari chairs would run $360–$540. That $200–$300 difference is real, but it transforms the visual impact of your shower.

## Step-by-Step: Planning Your Budget

**Step 1: Count your guests.**
This determines your table and chair count. A good rule: 5–6 guests per 60-inch round table, or 8–10 per banquet table.

**Step 3: Pick your linens and color scheme.**
One tablecloth color + one runner color = cohesive look. Pink runners are classic for baby showers, but baby blue and sage green are trending. Ivory or white tablecloths work with any runner color.

**Step 4: Calculate à la carte, then ask about bundles.**

**Step 5: Add delivery and any extras.**
Confirm delivery timing and any additional items (gift table setup, extra runners, etc.).
