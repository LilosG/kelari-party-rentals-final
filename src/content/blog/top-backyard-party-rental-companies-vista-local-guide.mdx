## Backyard Party Rental Pricing in Vista

Cost depends on several factors: guest count, table and chair selections, linen colors, and add-ons like runners or specialty items.

**Basic Setup (25–50 people)**
- 2–3 tables (mix of 6ft and 8ft banquet or 60-inch round)
- 15–25 chairs
- White or black linens
- Delivery and setup
- **Typical range: $350–$600**

**Mid-Size Celebration (50–100 people)**
- 4–6 tables
- 50–75 chairs
- Linens in your choice of colors (white, black, tan-ivory)
- Optional runners in seasonal colors (green, pink, baby blue, off-white, tan)
- Delivery and setup
- **Typical range: $600–$1,200**

**Large Event (100–200+ people)**
- 8–12 tables
- 100–150+ chairs
- Full linen package with runners
- Premium chair options (Chiavari chairs with 2-inch ivory cushions)
- Delivery, setup, and breakdown
- **Typical range: $1,200–$2,500+**

To compare apples to apples, ask each company: What's included in the quoted price? Are delivery and setup free or extra? Do they charge a damage deposit? What's the cancellation policy?

## Planning Your Vista Backyard Party: Key Logistics

**Measure Your Space**
Before you call, know your yard dimensions. Where will guests gather? Where will food go? Where can tables fit? A pro rental company will ask these questions and help you visualize the layout.

**Confirm Access**
Is there a gate? Will the delivery truck fit through it? Are there steps or uneven ground? Let your rental company know. We've set up hundreds of Vista backyards and know how to navigate tight spaces.

**Plan for Weather**
Vista summers are warm and dry. Spring can bring occasional rain. If weather is a concern, ask about tent rental options or choose a covered area of your yard. Linens in lighter colors (white, tan-ivory) reflect heat better than dark linens in summer.

**Timing Matters**
Delivery and setup typically take 30–60 minutes depending on party size. Schedule delivery at least 2 hours before guests arrive. That gives you time to troubleshoot and set out food.

**Guest Count Accuracy**
Overestimate slightly. It's better to have an extra chair than run short. Our team can advise on realistic table-to-guest ratios based on your event type.
