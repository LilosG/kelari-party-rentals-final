## Other Local Rental Options in Encinitas

While Kelari is our recommendation, here's a realistic look at other providers:

**Local Event Rental Companies**
Several general event rental firms serve Encinitas. Most stock standard inventory (basic folding chairs, plain tablecloths, generic tables). Pricing is competitive, but service varies. Some charge delivery fees ($75–$150 each way), require you to handle setup, or have limited availability on short notice. They're fine for no-frills events, but if you want personalized attention and premium finishes, you'll likely feel the difference.

**Big-Box Rental Chains**
National chains like Party City and Aaron's rent some furniture, but their focus is costumes, balloons, and party supplies—not tables and linens. Inventory is limited, quality is hit-or-miss, and you'll coordinate with multiple vendors. Not ideal for a cohesive baby shower look.

**DIY Approach**
Some hosts try Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist. You might find cheap chairs and tables, but condition is unpredictable, delivery is your problem, and there's no recourse if something breaks or doesn't show up. It's risky for an event you're investing time and money into.
