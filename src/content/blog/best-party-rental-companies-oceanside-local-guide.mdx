## How to Compare Party Rental Companies in Oceanside

When evaluating rental partners, use these criteria:

### Inventory Transparency
Ask for a detailed list of what's in stock. Can they show you chair styles, table sizes, and linen colors? If they're vague or require you to call back, that's a red flag. Kelari publishes its full inventory mix so you know exactly what's available before requesting a quote.

### Delivery Area & Fees
Confirm they serve Oceanside directly—not as an outlying zone with premium fees. Ask whether delivery is included in the quote or added separately. Request a flat rate for your specific venue to avoid surprises.

### Response Time
Contact them with a simple question (e.g., "Do you have 60" round tables available for June 15?"). Track how fast they respond. Local companies should reply within hours, not days.

### Flexibility & Customization
Real party planning often requires changes—a few extra chairs, a different linen color, or a last-minute date shift. Ask how they handle modifications. Companies that treat requests as complications rather than normal business are not worth the headache.

### Local References
Ask for recent Oceanside clients you can contact. Local testimonials carry more weight than generic online reviews because they speak to real Oceanside logistics and service quality.
